A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Something else on your mind?

Is Humeto just for education consultants?+

Education is our first focus. The planned shared applicant profile also supports individuals preparing study, visitor and Schengen applications, with a foundation for broader task management and CRM workflows.

Do students have to complete everything at once?+

No. The experience is designed around short, skippable sections. Students can upload first, save partial information and return later. Application-specific readiness highlights what is still needed.

How will application assistance work?+

The planned browser extension detects fields on the current university or visa page and suggests matches from approved profile information. Users review matches before filling. Unusual controls and uploads may need manual assistance. This website demonstrates the concept; it does not install an extension.

Can my team and students work together?+

Yes, that is the intended workflow: students provide information, consultants review evidence and both can see clear next steps. This preview uses fictional records and does not create real accounts.

Are the activity, reviews and pricing on this page real?+

They are illustrative demo content, including fictional people, agency names, testimonials, sample metrics and proposed prices. They show what a populated Humeto experience could feel like, rather than live usage or customer claims.
